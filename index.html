Step 2

Scheduling a Meeting with Clients

Follow these steps to correctly schedule a Teams meeting from the Calendar view in the desktop app.

📅Create & Send Invitation

  • 1
    Open Microsoft Teams
    Launch the desktop application
  • 2
    Go to Calendar
    Click Calendar icon in the left panel
  • 3
    Click New Meeting
    Top-right button in calendar view
  • 4
    Fill Meeting Details
    Title, Client email IDs, Counsellor/PSA email IDs, Date & Time, Agenda
  • 5
    Add Reminder
    Open reminder dropdown → Select "few minutes before"
  • 6
    Enable Auto Recording
    Go to Recording & Transcript → toggle on "Record Automatically" → Save settings
  • 7
    Click Send
    Invitation emails dispatched to all participants
Watch: Creating a New Meeting in Calendar

👥Add Co-organizer (Critical)

  • 1
    Add Counsellor/PSA as attendee
  • 2
    Click Meeting Options / More Options
  • 3
    Scroll to Roles → Choose Co-organizers
  • 4
    Select the Counsellor or PSA
  • 5
    Click Apply
Watch: Assigning Co-organizer During Meeting Options
🔄

Host disconnects? No problem

Co-organizer can manage the entire meeting if the original host loses connection.

🚪

Admit from lobby

Co-organizer can admit participants waiting in the lobby independently.

🎬

Continue recording

When LRD exits, co-organizer continues the recording without interruption.

Step 4

Best Practices During Client Meetings

Follow these guidelines to ensure every meeting is professional and distraction-free.

⏰
Join Early

Enter the meeting a few minutes before the scheduled start time to test your setup.

🔇
Mute When Silent

Keep microphone muted when you're not actively speaking to reduce background noise.

🎧
Use Headset

Use headphones with noise cancellation for better audio quality and fewer disturbances.

📡
Stable Internet

Connect via ethernet or strong Wi-Fi — avoid switching networks mid-meeting.

🪟
Share Selectively

Only share the specific screen or window required — not your entire desktop.

🏢
Professional Background

Ensure your background and environment look professional or use a virtual background.

Step 5

Recommended Meeting Flow

A structured workflow before, during, and after every client meeting.

⬤ Before Meeting
Schedule meeting in Teams Calendar
Add reminder notification
Assign co-organizer (Counsellor/PSA)
Verify audio & video setup
⬤ During Meeting
Admit client participants from lobby
Introduce all attendees
Share required screens/documents
Record only if authorized
⬤ After Meeting
Share recordings/resources if needed
Store data in OneDrive designated folder
Provide access to concerned stakeholders only
Section 7

Meeting Recording Expiration Policy

To manage storage efficiently and ensure compliance, all meeting recordings are subject to an expiration period set by the meeting owner.

90
Days

Short-Term Retention

Suitable for routine client meetings where recordings are reviewed soon after.

120
Days

Standard Retention

Default option for most meetings that may need review over a quarter.

∞
Never Expire

Permanent Retention

Only when long-term access to the recording is required. Use sparingly.

📋Guidelines

  • •
    Select expiration period based on business requirements and record retention needs
  • •
    Use Never Expire only when long-term access is genuinely required
  • •
    Review retention settings before sending the meeting invitation
  • •
    Ensure recordings comply with organizational policies and compliance requirements

Support

Frequently Asked Questions & Fixes

Common issues encountered when moving from Zoom to Teams, with tested solutions.

Screen sharing shows only the shared screen — client and PSA video feeds disappear
Screen Share
▾
  1. Use the Microsoft Teams desktop application — not the web browser.
  2. While sharing, enable Presenter Mode.
  3. Select Screen sharing (full screen) instead of a specific window.
Watch: Enabling Presenter Mode in Teams
Client can't enable camera — browser permission pop-up doesn't appear
Camera/Mic
▾
  1. Open the Teams meeting webpage.
  2. Click the Site Settings icon near the browser address bar.
  3. Navigate to Camera and Microphone permissions.
  4. Change both to Allow.
  5. Refresh the page and rejoin.
Watch: Fixing Browser Camera Permissions
Recording stops when LRD leaves (Zoom allowed LRD to exit after handing off host)
Recording
▾

Assign the Counsellor as Co-organizer before the meeting starts.

  1. Open Teams Calendar → open the scheduled meeting.
  2. Click Meeting Options.
  3. Find Choose Co-organizers, search and select the Counsellor.
  4. Save changes.
Flow: LRD joins → Counsellor joins as co-organizer → Counsellor starts recording → LRD exits → recording continues.
LRD's phone calls are being picked up by Teams microphone during meetings
Audio
▾

Use a separate mobile phone for dialing — not the same device connected to Teams.

  • Night Shift LRD: CloudTalk already installed on phones.
  • Day Shift LRD: Install CloudTalk on phones as well.
  • Domestic team: Use organization-provided phones with Airtel/Jio SIM.
No email notification when client joins early (Zoom sent one, Teams doesn't)
Notifications
▾

Use the Outlook desktop application instead of accessing Outlook through a browser. The desktop app provides better desktop email notifications that help track meeting activity more effectively.

Need centralized recording storage accessible to LOB managers
Storage
▾

A smart automation solution for centralized recordings is currently in development.

Interim approach: Each agent moves recordings to a specific OneDrive folder and provides access to the concerned stakeholder/team as required.
